# Watch a full game

## intro

This is one whole game, from the first turn to the score screen, sped up. The opponent is the very easy computer player. The kingdom has ten cards, and the strategy knows seven of them. It has never seen Vassal, Workshop or Bureaucrat, so it will never buy them. In the whole game it buys just one action card, a Smithy, on its very first turn.

## opening

Turn one, four coins. Smithy is at the top of the early buy list, so it buys Smithy. Turn two, three coins, a Silver. On turn three, Smithy draws into six coins, and it buys a Gold. On turn five, Smithy again, and eight coins buy the first Province.

## misread

Turn six shows the bug from the film about how it works. Smithy is in the hand, but the tilted card is read as a h l i w s. The game asks for an action. The autopilot reads the hand three times, finds no action card, and ends its action phase with two coins. After a Gold on turn seven, the autopilot was stopped, fixed, and started again. Its memory file let it carry on from turn eight.

## middle

From turn eight the reader works. Silver, Silver, and Silver again while the coins are low. On turn eleven, Smithy draws into twelve coins, and that buys a Province. Turns thirteen and fourteen bring two more. After fifteen turns it owns four of the eight provinces, and at turn sixteen the strategy moves into its late phase.

## late

The late phase changes what it buys. With four coins it now takes an Estate instead of a Silver. Turns sixteen, seventeen and eighteen all add Estates. On turn nineteen, Smithy finds eleven coins for a fifth Province. On turn twenty five the Estate pile is empty. The click does nothing and the coins don't change. The autopilot notices after that one click, and buys a Silver instead. Province number six comes on turn twenty nine.

## end

With two provinces left, the deck is full of victory cards and Copper. It buys a Duchy with five coins or more, Silver below that, and Copper with one or two. It never reaches eight coins again. So the game ends another way, on turn thirty nine. Three supply piles are empty, the Duchies, the Estates and the Gardens. The final score is fifty five to thirty five. A clear win, and a clear picture of the strategy's weak late game.
