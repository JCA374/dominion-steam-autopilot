Training log: the portfolio model

One model for any kingdom. A portfolio of specialists, each evolved on the Steam-rules engine with all 26 cards, on fresh random kingdoms that always hold its key cards. When Steam deals, the portfolio plays every specialist on that exact kingdom against the benchmark bots (about half a second) and lets the best one play the game.

Specialists

Score = win + ½ tie against Big Money, Smithy–Big Money and Witch–Big Money, on 60 fixed validation kingdoms per bot that hold the specialist's key cards (Steam rules, both seats). “Safe” runs never buy the nine cards the autopilot can't play yet.
